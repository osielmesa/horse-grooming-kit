## Overview

The 8-piece kit is the professional-grade version of the Masiel Spark equestrian grooming line. It expands the 4-piece essential set with four additional specialized tools — massage brush, sweat scraper, curry comb, and hoof pick — covering the full grooming routine from deep cleaning to finishing. All tools are stored in a larger handcrafted leather bag.
