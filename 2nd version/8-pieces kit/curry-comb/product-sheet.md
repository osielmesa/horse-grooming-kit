## Description

The Curry Comb has a distinctive **multi-ring coil design**: four concentric serrated metal rings of decreasing diameter, stacked and mounted on a **turned beechwood handle** with the MS logo laser-engraved. A central bolt holds the rings together and sets how freely they turn. This is a premium version of the traditional coil curry comb. Exclusive to the 8-piece set.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Adjustable ring tension, now shipped rigid.** The ring assembly turns on its central bolt, and the tension can be adjusted — tightened so the rings hold firm against the coat, or loosened so they rotate more freely. Every unit now leaves the factory **pre-set to the rigid setting**.

This is a direct response to customer feedback: buyers reported that rings turning too easily made the comb feel loose and reduced the pressure they could apply when breaking up caked dirt. Shipping it rigid means the comb works as expected straight out of the bag, and anyone who prefers a freer action can simply back the bolt off.

## Function & Use

- Loosening compacted dirt, mud, and dander deep in the coat
- Ring tension adjustable at the central bolt — ships rigid, loosen for a freer rotating action
- Removing shedding hair during seasonal coat change
- Used with circular or short back-and-forth strokes
- Used before body brushes in the grooming sequence
- Not recommended for use on legs, face, or bony areas
- Can also be used to clean the Dandy Brush by combing through the bristles
