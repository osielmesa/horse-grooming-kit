## Description

The Dandy Brush is the hard brush of the set — the first tool used on a dirty coat. It has a broad oval beechwood body with a dark brown leather hand strap fixed by two brass rivets, and short, densely packed **natural sisal bristles**.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Bristles upgraded to natural sisal.** The bristles are now natural sisal fiber, noticeably stiffer than the natural hair used previously. The added rigidity lifts caked mud, dried sweat, and embedded grit out of the coat in fewer strokes, so the horse spends less time being worked over. Sisal is a plant fiber, so the brush stays a fully natural product — and it is the traditional fiber for a dandy brush, which makes this a return to the authentic specification rather than a substitution.

The wooden body, leather strap, brass rivets, and 2.5 cm bristle length are unchanged.

## Function & Use

- First-stage grooming tool for removing heavy dirt and dried mud
- Used with firm flicking strokes to lift debris off the coat
- Not recommended for face or sensitive areas — use Massage Brush for those
- Suitable for body, neck, hindquarters, and legs
