## Description

The Hoof Pick pairs a steel hook pick with a stiff steel wire brush on a contoured beechwood handle, so a hoof can be cleared and swept out with one tool. The handle carries scalloped finger contours along its edge, a hanging hole at the base, and the "MS" logo laser-engraved on the face. Exclusive to the 8-piece kit.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Handle reshaped for a better grip.** The handle has been redesigned to sit more comfortably in the hand. The grip width was reduced from 43 mm to 37 mm and the profile behind the finger scallops was slimmed, so the hand closes fully around the handle instead of stretching over it — a meaningful difference when picking out four hooves in a row, or working with cold or gloved hands.

The overall tool is also slightly more compact than the previous version. The change was selected from three prototype profiles.

> Three separate width figures apply to this tool and are easy to confuse. **7.8 cm** is the overall width including the wire brush that projects sideways from the head. **3.7 cm** is the handle width at the finger scallops — the figure that changed in the redesign, from 43 mm. **2.05 cm** is the handle thickness, the dimension the hand pinches.
>
> The grip narrowing does not appear in the overall width because only the grip was slimmed; the head where the brush mounts was left alone.

## Function & Use

- Removing compacted mud, stones, and bedding from the hoof cavity
- Cleaning around the frog and bars of the hoof
- Integrated brush sweeps out loosened debris after picking
- Essential pre-ride and post-ride hoof care tool
- Helps identify thrush, stones, or hoof damage during use
