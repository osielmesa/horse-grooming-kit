## Description

The Mane & Tail Pin Brush detangles and smooths the mane and tail using ball-tipped metal pins set in a flexible rubber cushion pad. It has a long beechwood paddle handle with a leather hanging loop and the "MS / MASIEL SPARK" logo laser-engraved on the back of the head.

---

## What's New in This Version

**Improved pin tips.** The ball tips on the pins have been upgraded to a smoother, more consistently finished head. Rough or uneven tips are what catch and tear long hair, so the better finish lets the brush pass through a thick tail without snagging — less breakage at the ends and less hair pulled out with each stroke. This matters most on tails, where hair that breaks off takes months to grow back.
