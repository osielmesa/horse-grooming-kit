## Overview

The 8-piece kit is the professional-grade version of the Masiel Spark equestrian grooming line. It expands the 4-piece essential set with four additional specialized tools — massage brush, sweat scraper, curry comb, and hoof pick — covering the full grooming routine from deep cleaning to finishing. All tools are stored in a larger handcrafted leather bag.

## What's New in This Version

Four improvements over the previous release:

| Item | Improvement |
|------|-------------|
| **Dandy Brush** | Bristles upgraded to **natural sisal** — a stiffer plant fiber that lifts caked mud and dried sweat in fewer strokes, while keeping the brush a fully natural product. Sisal is also the traditional fiber for a dandy brush. |
| **Hoof Pick** | Handle **reshaped for a more comfortable grip**, narrowed from 43 mm to 37 mm across the finger contours so the hand closes fully around it. |
| **Mane & Tail Pin Brush** | **Improved ball tips** on the pins — a smoother, more consistent finish that passes through a thick tail without snagging, reducing hair breakage. |
| **Curry Comb** | **Ring tension is now adjustable** and every unit ships pre-set to the rigid setting, answering customer feedback that freely spinning rings reduced the pressure that could be applied. |

For the dandy the wooden body is 9 cm rather than 10; for the massage brush, 7.5 cm rather than 9. Use the rivet-to-rivet figure for packing calculations and the body figure for artwork.
