# Soft Finishing Brush

**Set:** Masiel Spark Premium Horse Grooming Kit (8 Pieces)  
**ASIN (Set):** B0GBZJVY8B

---

## Description

The Soft Finishing Brush is the last brush in the grooming sequence. It has an **elongated beechwood body with tapered, pointed ends** and no hand strap — held directly in the palm. The bristles are uniformly white, long, and very soft. The "MS" logo is laser-engraved on the back of the body. It works alongside the Dandy Brush and Massage Brush to complete a full grooming sequence.

---

## Visual Details (from product photos)

- Elongated body with tapered, pointed ends — a leaf or boat outline, not the broad oval of the Dandy Brush
- Pure white / cream uniform bristles, very soft
- Natural light beechwood color — no dark stain
- "MS" logo laser-engraved on the back of the body
- No leather hand strap — palm-grip design
- Softly rounded edges along the whole body

## Function & Use

- Final-stage coat polishing after the Dandy Brush and Massage Brush
- Removes fine dust and distributes natural coat oils
- Brings out shine on coat, neck, and hindquarters
- Soft enough for daily use and sensitive skin areas
